## Local adaptations to preserve

- The [library-wide adaptations](../../agents/maintain-skills/references/ADAPTATION.md#library-wide-adaptations) apply; this list holds only this skill's own divergences.
- Local-only files: `MAINTENANCE.md`, `VALIDATION.md`, and `suite.json`, the Pstack mapping that `/maintain-skills` reads; `references/plan-template.md`, the plan template that upstream keeps inline in its multi-phase-plan playbook; `scripts/worktree-audit.py` with `scripts/tests/test_worktree_audit.py`, which replace upstream's `scripts/worktree-audit.sh` with a read-only audit over host-neutral activity and ownership indexes instead of Cursor transcripts and sidebar state; `scripts/orch/lock.ts` with `lock.test-helper.ts`, `store-lock.test.ts`, `store-concurrency.test.ts`, `cli-cleanup.test.ts`, and `cli-cleanup.test-helper.ts`, the serialized lock and Store fixes listed below; and `scripts/bootstrap.test.ts` with `scripts/tsconfig.json`, which test and typecheck the immutable-source runtime.
- Playbooks are condensed into ordered steps with completion criteria, and Cursor mechanics (`poteto-agent`, `Task` defaults, fixed model slugs, `/loop`, `AskQuestion`, `cursor-team-kit` control skills, `/deslop`, Cursor cloud agents, Origin) are translated per the library-wide host adaptation. Role models come from /pstack-setup's configuration through /pstack-swarm. Shared owners take over their disciplines: /diagnosing-bugs, /tdd, /domain-modeling, /review-loop for the final review, and /pr for PR descriptions. Feature, Perf issue, Bug fix, Hillclimb, and Refactoring carry every upstream rule in upstream's wording where it survives translation, including the four-item throughput checkpoint, mandatory review-separated delegation of code-writing, the single-owner rule, small ordered commits, and the ordered performance mantras.
- Opening a PR opens draft PRs and follows the repository's commit-title and PR conventions, where upstream opens ready PRs with Conventional Commits titles and its own section list, per the library-wide authority adaptation and the /pr owner.
- Share phase-specific principle selection with Matt entrypoints through the locally authored /pstack-principles owner. Preserve explicit mode activation and keep suite.json as a source mapping rather than a second selection policy. The refactoring playbook uses the migration owner for compatibility and verification gates.
- Keep one maintenance engine in `/maintain-skills`; `suite.json` remains this family's data source. Include category/shared files inside upstream skill roots.
- Preserve explicit review and unknown/blocked merge gates, cycle-safe stack traversal, forge-supplied immutable PR heads, active-over-historical PR selection, and verified trunk resolution. Hold an unverified Origin head API instead of substituting local refs.
- Keep polling sleeps, retries, and queued partial sweeps within the configured deadline. In-flight queries finish; this option does not cancel subprocesses.
- Serialize orchestration lock mutations and identify leases independently of PID. Recover legacy PID-only locks and dead mutation guards; an earlier handle cannot release its successor. Force remains an explicit main-lease override.
- Serialize operations admitted through each Store handle, preserving call-order visibility and draining accepted work before close releases the lease.
- Keep a failed close retryable without reopening the store to operations. Retain its release identity until cleanup succeeds and share concurrent close attempts. CLI cleanup failures warn without changing the operation result or hiding its primary error.
- Scope lock ownership to updated cooperating writers on one host. PID reuse or unavailable process probing conservatively blocks automatic guard reclamation.
