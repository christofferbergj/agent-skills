## Local adaptations to preserve

The wording is first-party. These are the principles taken from the source and the deliberate departures.

Adopted principles:

- The glance: a headline of at most 12 words that opens with a traffic-light marker and the bold outcome in terms of what the user asked for, with an answer to a question there as a best guess and how sure you are; one shape view of code or design work; up to three ranked notes of at most 15 words; one fold for what the user may audit or compare; and a 60-word budget for the headline and notes. How to act on a question is behavior, which /next-options holds, as upstream's router does.
- A waiting line, mid-turn too, opens with ⏳ and names what it waits on, how long it has run, about how long is left, and what comes next.
- Notes for the user open with Decide, Review, or Do, and a Do shows once, in the reply that first needs it.
- A decision card of about twelve lines: a yes-or-no question, the problem, one view by the kind of call (design, product, message, ship gate, UI, or stuck), the pick with what it optimizes and its source, the cost only when the pick is hard to undo, and one fold; `a` is yes and `b` is no, the smallest option.
- The writing rules: plain words, one picture per turn with the shape counting as one, inline code only for a name the user would type or search, each piece shown once except drafts shown again in full in the reply that posts or sends them, and the pre-send check that reads only the headline, shape, notes, and ending.
- Names, not numbers: each item is called by its short name, and only a list in this reply that the user might answer by number is numbered.
- A skill's own report fills the glance, a skill that names its own format for a call keeps it, and a catch-up shows every waiting call past the notes limit and budget; its screenshots, videos, and diagrams follow /ketchup's rules instead of the picture limit, and /ketchup owns the catch-up itself.

Departures:

- The ending belongs to /next-options, so this skill keeps only the closing rule above it and defines no next-step or Done format of its own. Upstream's closing, with its one-line Done and its ⏳ headline with a `then:` clause, is decided in /next-options' record; its redirect of a reply that waits only on a merge go to the ship gate card is met here by the card's trigger.
- The views and pictures references and the Mermaid checker are left out to keep the rules small enough to load at every session start; the picture rule, the shape, and each kind of card view keep a line each.
- The Views rule keeps `show-me`'s pseudocode for logic and real code only when most of it is new or copyable. Upstream's shape now leads code work with an interface and call site, a tree, or a flow, which this rule adopts, but prefers typed code throughout.
- The layout shapes the reply that ends each turn, where Oliver's shapes every reply as a report or a card. A mid-turn progress update is one sentence without parts, as upstream now also keeps it to one line, and the waiting rule above still applies to it.
- The rules load through /next-options' SessionStart hook in a second entry with its own hook budget, rather than a second script.
- A Decide note still carries a choice that doesn't shape code or the plan, with its options in the fold, and the decision card stays for one that does or for a go on a merge, deploy, or message. Upstream gives every Decide its own queued card, to spend the glance where judgement matters; here that would spend a turn on each small choice.
- After a compaction, a lost name is looked up. Upstream's names-not-numbers rule has no such line, since its `bare_references.py` hook catches a bare number, and this library ships no such hook.
- Errors keep the matter-of-fact cause-and-fix line from the i-have-adhd ancestor, which upstream's NOTICE still lists although the glance rewrite dropped its line.
- A /teach explanation's diagram series is exempt from the picture limit (owner decision, 2026-10-27), because /teach builds a system up one diagram at a time in one explanation, and a series split across turns stops reading as one picture assembling.
- The catch-up exemption stays (owner decision, 2026-10-21), now from the notes limit and budget as well as the picture limit. Upstream removed its copy because ketchup's own skill states those rules, but without it the loaded limits would contradict /ketchup's rules in every session.

`agents/openai.yaml` and this record are locally maintained.
